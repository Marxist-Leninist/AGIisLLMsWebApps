τ = 1 − t. Values are unity-normalised asymptotic ratios relative to t = 0.9, not dimensional measurements. This is a near-event window, not the manuscript’s full evolution from rest. The illustrative choice h = 0.006 obeys the headline range 0 < h < 0.01 but is not a certified choice for every smallness requirement of the construction. Aspect-ratio change is deliberately not exaggerated.

What the browser actually computes

A 64 × 64 × 64 virtual dye field is transported on the GPU with midpoint backtracing and semi-Lagrangian interpolation. The prescribed axisymmetric surrogate is divergence-free analytically: its radial and axial velocities come from one Stokes streamfunction, with an axisymmetric swirl added. The dye evolves in shrinking similarity coordinates, including the coordinate-drift terms. The shrinking window is an Eulerian observation region, not a shrinking material parcel.

The viewport uses volumetric absorption, shadowing, warm/cool studio illumination and procedural fine detail. Colour and dye injection reveal motion; they are not water density, measured temperature or pressure. The optical exposure is normalised for visibility. Scrubbing reseeds the virtual dye. Playback is render-paced: it slows on a device that cannot draw frames quickly enough, rather than skipping unresolved steps. The laboratory-style floor is visual staging, not a wall boundary condition.

What is not solved here

The exact E and U profiles, smooth forcing, heat exterior and infinite residual-correction sequence are not instantiated. No momentum residual or proof-verification result is being reported. The pulse overlay uses the reference envelope (7.12) with illustrative parameters λ₀ = 1, u* = 2, Lₛ = 24; the locations and colours do not reconstruct the complete pulse field or its stress cancellation. Tracer-grid diffusion is numerical/visual, not a measurement of physical viscosity.

The closest physical analogue

A strained Burgers vortex has inward radial flow, rotation and outward axial stretching. In its steady form, stretching balances viscous diffusion; its core does not collapse to a singularity. The comparison mode evaluates this steady prescribed velocity field, with normalised a = 1.3, ν = a/4, Γ = 10π and core parameter 1. It is neither a finite-energy whole-space flow nor the manuscript’s construction. A laboratory apparatus using two co-rotating disks and axial withdrawal was described by Webster and Young (2015).

At the limit

The approach stops at τ = 10⁻⁷, before t = 1. The limit overlay identifies the manuscript’s singular time; it does not render infinity or a physical explosion. “Follow core” magnifies the shrinking observation region; “Fixed scale” reveals its actual relative contraction. In analogue mode the singular-time clock is disabled and the steady vortex simply continues.
