Solar System Gravity Sim: Newton vs Light-Speed Delay

Side-by-side toy simulation of a Sun-Earth system. Left uses ordinary instantaneous Newtonian gravity. Right uses a naive retarded version where the Earth feels the Sun at a past time determined by light-travel delay. Because apparently one clean theory wasn’t enough for the species.

Single-file HTML
Units are normalized to keep this stable in-browser:
• distance unit = 1 AU
• time unit = 1 second internally, displayed in days/years
• speed of light slider is in multiples of the real light speed used by the delayed model
Newton: a(t) = -GM r(t) / |r(t)|^3 Delayed toy model: a(t) = -GM [x_E(t) - x_S(t_r)] / |x_E(t) - x_S(t_r)|^3 with t - t_r = |x_E(t) - x_S(t_r)| / c_g
Notes: the delayed panel is the naive retarded-force model you asked for, not General Relativity. Its purpose is to show the lag/aberration effect directly. In normal Earth-Sun conditions the real light delay is only about 499 seconds, so the visible difference is small unless you exaggerate it.
