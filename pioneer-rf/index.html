WHAT IS REAL / WHAT IS SYNTHETIC
Historical inputs

Pioneer 6 mass 62.14 kg; S-band downlink around 2292 MHz; 8 W transmitter; 11.2 dBi spacecraft antenna; telemetry rates down to 8 bit/s; spin near 60 rpm.

Physics

Friis free-space path loss, parabolic-aperture gain, Boltzmann thermal noise, C/N₀, Eb/N₀, BPSK AWGN BER, Shannon capacity and finite light time.

Learned part

24×16 log-power waterfall → one-layer multi-head transformer → 9 drift actions. Offline warm-start + REINFORCE fine-tune; browser continues REINFORCE on the actor head.

Simulation boundary

RFI, frame/pilot envelope, oscillator drift and online rewards are stochastic synthetic data. No claim is made that Pioneer 6 is currently transmitting.
