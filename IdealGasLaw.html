PV = nRT  → 
Tip: drag the piston up/down on the cylinder to change it directly.

R = 0.082057 L·atm·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹. In Free piston mode you set P, n, T and the gas finds its volume (V = nRT/P). In Locked piston mode you set V, n, T and the pressure builds up (P = nRT/V). Particle speed tracks temperature, particle count tracks the amount of gas.
