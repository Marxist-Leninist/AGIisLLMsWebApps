3D QFT Volumetric Renderer

PROBABILITY DOESN'T EXIST: No random Monte Carlo rays are cast. This engine performs a full 3D volumetric evaluation of the deterministic Feynman Path Integral.

For every step in the 3D fog, the shader sums the complex quantum phase of light bouncing off every point on the mirror simultaneously. The straight beam is pure constructive interference.

Click and drag to rotate camera. Scroll to zoom.

Moving off -0.75 destroys the phase interference (defocus).
Lower values run faster but introduce aliasing.
